FINDING: Fusion did not improve over RoBERTa alone, suggesting RoBERTa's
contextual embeddings already capture most of the useful signal, while
simple mean-pooled GloVe vectors add mostly redundant/noisy information.

LIMITATION OBSERVED
--------------------
The model struggles with indirect/implicit hate speech that lacks explicit
slurs or aggressive language (e.g. the ethnicity example above), consistent
with the "contextual ambiguity" limitation noted in the reference paper.
